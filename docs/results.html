Detection rate and precision come from the rules scorer, so they cannot drift from the figures published elsewhere. Conversion is reported as measurable or not, never as zero: an unworked queue and a queue whose every alert was cleared are opposite facts.

The finding hiding in these five numbers

The ceiling on alert-to-SAR conversion is exactly queue precision. If analysts never erred, every alert on a laundering account would become a filing and every other would be cleared — so the industry's headline analyst KPI collapses, at best, into a property of the queue. In production the two are inseparable, because nobody knows which cleared alerts were mistakes.

What is deliberately not shown

Conversion reads not measurable, never 0%. No case in this world has been worked to a disposition, and “nobody has reviewed these” and “everything reviewed was cleared” are opposite facts that a zero would merge into the one that makes a detection stack look broken.

Experiments

Three measurements, in full

Each one states its objective, shows the chart, then answers what it found, why it matters, how it works and what it means operationally.

Phase 3

Rules engine — recall by typology

Six tunable scenarios, each aimed at one typology's actual transaction signature. The question: what share of each crime type does a conventional rules engine actually catch?

Recall per injected typology, scored against ground truth. Overall 86.1% recall at 65.3% precision across 36 schemes. A short bar is a measured blind spot, not a missing measurement.

Key finding

Recall is not uniform across crime types. A short bar is a measured blind spot, not a missing measurement — small structuring genuinely looks like a shop banking its takings.

Why it matters

A single aggregate recall number hides exactly this. A bank reporting “86% recall” upward may be at 100% on one typology and near zero on another, and nobody in the room would know which.

Technical explanation

These thresholds were re-tuned twice. The original figures scored 100% precision — but only because the synthetic world emitted no legitimate cash at all, so any cash deposit was crime. Once merchants and businesses banked real takings, structuring_burst went from 0 to 24 false positives on a clean world. The thresholds now sit in the measured gap between legitimate cash-banking (tops out ~21 deposits) and structuring (27–47).

Business implication

Rule coverage should be reported per typology in MI packs, not as one number. The aggregate is the number that gets presented; the per-typology breakdown is the one that tells you where you are blind.

Phase 5

Graph analytics — what a single bank can see

Rebuild the internal transfer graph by pairing the two legs of each payment, then look for pass-through chains. The question: how much of each typology is even visible to a graph?

Share of each typology that leaves any internal edge to analyse. The zeros are the cross-bank blind spot, not a detection failure: those counterparties bank elsewhere, so the scheme leaves one leg and no edge. Of the chains that do exist, the detector found 8/8 at 100% precision.

Key finding

Only one of six typologies leaves an internal edge at all. The other five have counterparties outside the bank, so they leave one leg and no edge — there is nothing for a graph to analyse.

Why it matters

Graph analytics is sold as a general AML capability. Measured here, its reach is bounded by a structural fact about where counterparties bank, not by the quality of the algorithm.

Technical explanation

Edges are reconstructed on the reference number shared by both legs, not on (timestamp, amount) — the latter cross-pairs unrelated coincident transfers. Chains are suffix-collapsed, because growing from every edge rediscovers a long chain from its 2nd and 3rd accounts and an investigator wants the path once. Fan-in/fan-out detectors were built and then deleted: they fired zero times at usable thresholds, and 72 of 76 hits when loosened were ordinary merchants.

Business implication

This is the direct setup for the cross-bank experiment. If five of six typologies are invisible to a graph inside one bank, the case for inter-bank intelligence sharing is not ideological — it is the only way those edges come into existence.

Phase 7

Workbench queue — which layer reaches an analyst

Four detection layers feed one investigator queue. The question: which layer actually puts work in front of a human, and which one merely contributes evidence to somebody else's case?

50 open cases. The top four bars are which tier a case was filed under; the lower four are how many cases each layer contributed any evidence to. A layer can carry many cases without ever owning a tier.

Key finding

A layer can carry many cases without ever owning a tier. Filing tier and contribution are different measurements, and only showing the first would credit the wrong control.

Why it matters

If you report “the model found 24 cases” when the model merely agreed with a rule that found them, you will fund the wrong thing at the next budget round.

Technical explanation

Tier order is by how specific a reason the analyst gets — a graph names a path, a rule names a scenario, screening names a person, a model names nothing. It is deliberately not ordered by standalone precision, which measures each layer as a lone ranker and once filed sanctions hits under a “model-ranked” heading.

Business implication

The model tier is empty here by arithmetic and on purpose: a model-only alert tops out below the case-opening threshold, because an alert with no explainable reason should not consume an analyst's day.

Limitations

What these numbers do not say

The world is synthetic

Its realism bounds every figure here. Four separate findings in this project were corrections to artefacts of this generator; others certainly remain.

One world, one seed

These are a real measured example, not a population statistic. Averaging over seeds is future work.

counterparty_concentration is knowingly imprecise

Measured as unfixable by threshold: shell schemes sit at 6 payments and 50% concentration, inside the legitimate 5–9 and 51–63% range. An honest supplier with one big client and a shell-fed front are not separable on concentration alone.

Small structuring is a documented blind spot

Genuinely indistinguishable from a shop banking its takings — which is precisely why real structuring works.
