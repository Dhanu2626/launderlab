Detection rate and precision come from the rules scorer, so they cannot drift from the figures published elsewhere. Conversion is reported as measurable or not, never as zero: an unworked queue and a queue whose every alert was cleared are opposite facts. Its ceiling equals queue precision exactly — which means the industry's headline analyst KPI reduces, at best, to a property of the queue rather than of the analyst.
