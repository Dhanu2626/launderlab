Mule-chain hops reconstructed, by view

Share of planted mule-chain hops each view can reconstruct. A single bank sees essentially none of the network no matter how the chain was placed - rebuilding a chain needs consecutive hops inside one bank, which is rare once accounts are spread across institutions. Co-operation recovers most of it while sharing only hashed payment references, never customer data.
